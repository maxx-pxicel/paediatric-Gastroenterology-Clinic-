Dr Hnin Mya Swe

Dr Hnin Mya Swe is a consultant paediatrician and paediatric gastroenterologist. As an experienced paediatrician with more than 10 years clinical experience, she specialises in treating children with general medical conditions. Her clinical expertise also includes management of children with gastrointestinal conditions as well as performing endoscopic procedures.
